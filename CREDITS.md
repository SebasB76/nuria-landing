## Made for this project

- `site/assets/img/cerro-santa-ana.svg`: the Cerro Santa Ana illustration, generated by
  `tools/cerro_santa_ana.py`.
- `site/assets/img/nuria-portrait-960.webp` and `site/assets/img/nuria-head-288.webp`: Nuria's
  official character art and its head crop, the same files the Nuria app uses.
- The brand mark in `site/index.html`, `site/assets/icon.svg` and `site/assets/apple-touch-icon.png`:
  the risk drop drawn as Nuria's head, with her crest and long bill.
- The inline risk drops in `site/index.html`, adapted from the Nuria app's own icons.
